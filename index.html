Prices here are mid-range estimates for Bengaluru, put together from published rates and what these places actually cost through 2025 and 2026. GST sits on top of service charge, the same order a printed bill uses. Booking fees, camera charges, parking and arcade cards are all counted, because that is how a ₹300 evening quietly turns into ₹520.

Getting there and back is shown under every total but kept out of your cap, since the app has no idea where you are starting from. Tick "Count the travel" and it goes in.

What this is not is live pricing. Menus move, turf goes up on a Friday, and somewhere that was ₹250 last year is ₹340 now. Treat the total as a bracket, and ring ahead for anything that needs booking.

How this works

Every group has the same conversation. Somebody asks where to go, four people say "anywhere", and three days later nobody went anywhere.

Most arguments about where to go are really arguments about money that nobody wants to start out loud. So the budget goes first here. The rest gets easier after that.

You get three options. Not forty. Groups get slower as the list gets longer, so there is a reroll button instead of a scroll.

Every card carries the whole bill: tax, service charge, booking fees, shoe rental, the arcade card you will definitely end up buying. The big number is what actually leaves your account.

Then you vote. Pass the phone round the table, or send one link. Nobody installs anything. Ties go to the cheaper plan.

Saved plans live in this browser and nowhere else. No account, no server, nothing to delete later.
